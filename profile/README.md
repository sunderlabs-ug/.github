# Sunderlabs

**Robot learning that transfers: across partners, tasks, and robot bodies.**

We are an applied AI lab for physical systems. We build fast physics environments, train and evaluate robot policies, and study hardware designs.

Our method is simple: profile the full loop, improve the bottleneck, and measure the result.

### What we work on

- **Simulation and environments** — custom physics environments for robot learning and evaluation.
- **Performance engineering** — profiling and optimization across physics, sensors, inference, and training.
- **Robot policies** — repeatable experiments, baseline comparisons, and evaluation across tasks and robot bodies.
- **Hardware design** — physics-driven design studies and engineering tools for comparing candidates.

### Work with us

We help robotics and hardware teams through scoped performance audits, environment builds, and policy development pilots.
